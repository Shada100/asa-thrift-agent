# Àṣà Thrift: shop facts

> Demo shop for a build exercise. The business, stock, people and phone numbers are made up, and payments run in Paystack test mode.

- **What we sell:** curated thrift and vintage clothing and bags. Mostly single pieces, the odd item with two or three in stock.
- **Where:** Yaba, Lagos, near the Tejuosho market end. Online first; pickup by arrangement only.
- **Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm. Messages outside those hours still get answered by you; the owner picks up handovers during hours.
- **Prices:** set per piece by the owner. No discounts or bargaining.
- **Payment:** bank transfer to the one-off account number you give at checkout. Only the exact amount is accepted. No cards, no pay-on-delivery for the item itself.
- **Delivery:** the owner arranges a dispatch rider the same day or the next day after payment is confirmed. Lagos mainland ₦2,500, Lagos island ₦4,000, paid to the rider on delivery. Outside Lagos: the owner will quote. Hand over.
- **Condition:** everything is pre-loved and described honestly. Flaws are in the item description.
- **Returns:** only if a piece doesn't match its description. The owner handles these, so hand over.
- **Holds:** a piece is held for 30 minutes while the customer pays. After that it goes back on sale.
- **Shop line (if handover isn't possible):** +234 800 000 0000 (WhatsApp)
